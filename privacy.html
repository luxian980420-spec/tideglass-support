Tideglass Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026. Tideglass is provided by jiashuai sun.

The game works offline and does not require an account. We do not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party advertising SDKs in the game. There are no in-app purchases.

Your level progress, shells, coins, settings, and recent game state are stored locally on your device. The app does not send this game data to us or to a game server.

Removing the app deletes its local save. Device backups and any restoration of those backups are managed by Apple under your device settings and Apple's privacy practices. Tideglass does not provide its own cloud sync.

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to answer and resolve your request, not for advertising or profiling. Avoid including sensitive information that is not needed. You can request deletion of your support correspondence using the contact address below; we keep correspondence only as long as needed for support or a legal obligation.

The game does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location, or advertising identifier.

Support and privacy links open a website outside the game. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives ordinary web-request information such as your IP address and browser details. We do not add analytics scripts or tracking cookies to these pages. Hosting is subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. If a child sends personal information in a support email, a parent or guardian may contact us to request its deletion.

Any changes to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date.

For privacy questions or requests, contact jiashuai sun at 992383479@qq.com.
