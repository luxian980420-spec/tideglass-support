Shellshore Sort Support

For help with Shellshore Sort, contact jiashuai sun at 992383479@qq.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, level number, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords or payment details.

How do I move shells?

Tap a shell, then tap a destination string, or drag it there. Match shell colors and fill a basket to collect it. The first levels introduce the rules.

What if I get stuck?

Use Undo to step back, Hint for a suggested move, or Add String for more room. Restart begins the current level again. Some strings unlock after you collect the shells shown on their lock.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is stored on your device. There is no account or in-game cloud sync. Removing the app deletes its local save, so please contact support before reinstalling if you want to keep your progress. Device backups are handled by Apple.

How do I change sound or vibration?

Open Settings using the gear icon. Sound and Haptics can be switched on or off separately.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Levels, collections, and coasts work offline. Opening these support and privacy pages or sending an email requires an internet connection.
